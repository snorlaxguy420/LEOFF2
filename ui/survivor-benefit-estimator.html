Survivor Benefit Estimator

Compare the tradeoff between more pension while you are alive and more pension protection for your spouse after your death. This tool is built to make the math more visible, not to predict when anyone will die.

Best For

Spouse-protection tradeoffs

How To Read This

  • Your monthly pension is what you keep while you are alive.
  • Survivor monthly pension is what your spouse would keep after your death.
  • The death-age table assumes the survivor still needs the pension for the selected number of years after your death.
  • Single life usually maximizes current income. Higher survivor percentages usually protect the household more if you die earlier.
Estimator basis

The joint-option reductions here are estimated from sampled Washington DRS outputs across spouse age gaps from 10 years younger to 10 years older. We scale those reductions to your single-life pension and interpolate between age gaps.

What this is not

This is not a mortality prediction model and it is not a replacement for an official DRS quote. It is a decision aid designed to help you feel the tradeoff range more clearly.
