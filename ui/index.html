What is LEOFF Helper?

LEOFF Helper was built by and for Washington LEOFF 2 members to simplify retirement planning. It now brings together retirement tools, pension forecasting, long-form guides, and plan modeling so members can move from learning the rules to testing real retirement decisions.

It is no longer just a single calculator. It is becoming a planning platform: compare retirement ages, model full retirement scenarios, read deeper guides, and see where your plan looks strong or fragile before making major decisions.

Decision Tools

Use focused pages like the Retirement Age Comparison Tool, Survivor Benefit Estimator, and Lifetime Pension Value Calculator to compare retirement timing, service credit, pension value, and household tradeoffs side by side.

Pension Forecasting

Project your LEOFF pension using service credit, final average salary, benefit enhancement choices, and retirement timing so you can see how the formula changes as work years build.

Guides and Articles

Read deeper guides on LEOFF pension rules, bridge years, survivor options, and retirement tradeoffs when you want more than a quick estimate or need help understanding the why behind the math.

Forecasting and Planning

Model pensions, Social Security, assets, expenses, and vulnerabilities together to see how durable your retirement plan really is and where it may be most vulnerable.
