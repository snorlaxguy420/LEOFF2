Core Philosophy

LEOFF 2 provides a strong defined benefit pension. However, relying solely on one income source may expose retirees to structural risks.

  • Inflation risk
  • COLA underperformance
  • Legislative risk
  • Longevity risk
  • Healthcare cost escalation

A resilient retirement structure may include diversified income streams such as 401(k), 457(b), real estate, brokerage investments, Treasury securities, precious metals, and other diversified assets.

This calculator is designed to highlight potential structural gaps. It is not a substitute for comprehensive professional advice.
