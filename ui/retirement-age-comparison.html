Why it matters

This focuses on the financial power of working longer: more salary years, more service credit, a larger pension multiplier, and less time to bridge before Social Security starts.

Tiered multiplier math used here

Base pension = FAS x service years x 2.0%

If `Tiered Multiplier` is selected, the tool adds: 0.5% x FAS for each service year above 15, up to 25 years total.

That means the bonus applies only to service years 16 through 25.

What this LEOFF retirement age comparison tool measures

This tool compares retirement ages side by side using LEOFF-specific pension inputs: birth year, LEOFF start year, final average salary, benefit enhancement choice, and Social Security claim age. It highlights service credit, annual pension, monthly pension, percentage of FAS, bridge years, and lifetime pension value through your selected target age.

Why comparing retirement ages matters for LEOFF members

For many Washington LEOFF members, the real decision is not just whether retirement is possible. It is whether waiting a few more years materially changes pension value, percentage of FAS, and the number of years before Social Security begins. This page is designed to make those tradeoffs visible quickly.

When to use this tool versus the full calculator

Use this comparison page when you want a clean side-by-side pension timing view. Use the full retirement calculator when you want to bring in expenses, assets, withdrawals, and risk. Then use the retirement dashboard to review the broader plan by retirement age.
